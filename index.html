Frequently asked questions

How are the lunar dates calculated?
On your device, from astronomical new moons and solar terms, for 1901–2099. The dates are checked against the calendar tables published by the Hong Kong Observatory.

Why does my lunar date differ from another calendar by one day?
A lunar month starts on the day of the new moon in local time. China, Hong Kong and Taiwan count in UTC+8, Vietnam in UTC+7 and Korea in UTC+9, so a few dates differ. Choose your calendar in Settings → Lunar calendar.

Where do “Suitable” and “Avoid” come from?
From the traditional almanac rules: the twelve day officers (Jian-Chu), the yellow and black path, and the clash of the day sign. The app shows what the traditional almanac prints for the day; it is a cultural reference, not advice.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → find the app in the list. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → add a widget.

What does Premium include?
The full day notes, hours and directions for any date and the complete list in the day finder. Today, the month grid, solar terms, festivals and widgets are free.

How do I restore my purchase?
Settings → Restore purchases, or the same button on the Premium screen.

How do I cancel the subscription?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the renewal date.

Where is my data?
Only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
