Privacy Policy

Updated 22 November 2026 · effective from version 1.1 · applies to the Chinese Calendar: Tong Shu app for iPhone

Short version: your settings and saved days stay on your iPhone. From version 1.1 the app collects usage statistics and crash reports to help us improve it. There are no accounts and no ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What stays on your device

The days you save in the day finder and which birth-year sign you chose are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone and in the storage it shares with its own widgets. They are never sent to us or to our analytics provider.

Usage statistics and crash reports

From version 1.1 we use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how the app is used. It receives:

  • Actions in the app, such as screens and sections opened, months browsed, the kind of occasion picked in the day finder (for example “wedding” or “moving”), how far from today an opened day is (as a range, not the date), and taps on widgets.
  • General settings, such as app language, the calendar you chose (China, Vietnam or Korea), whether a birth-year sign is set (not which one), whether Chinese terms are shown, and which widget sizes are installed.
  • Purchase activity, such as the plan, price, currency, and whether a purchase, free trial or restore started, was cancelled or failed.
  • Technical data, such as an app instance identifier generated by Firebase, device model, iOS and app version, App Store country, approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and freeze reports.

This data is not used for advertising. The app does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more about privacy and security in Firebase. Version 1.0 of the app contains no analytics.

Calculations

Lunar dates, solar terms, festivals and the almanac notes are calculated on your device from the date. The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, calendar or any other permission.

Purchases

The Premium subscription and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks the purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, the app's data is included in your backup under your Apple Account settings, as for other apps.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can remove saved days in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. To ask about or delete usage statistics linked to your app instance, write to support.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
