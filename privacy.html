Privacy Policy

Updated 21 November 2026 · applies to the Chinese Calendar: Tong Shu app for iPhone, version 1.0

Short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It works without an internet connection. Your settings and saved days are stored only on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive or sell any personal data.

What stays on your device

The calendar you choose (China, Vietnam or Korea), the optional birth-year sign, the “Show Chinese terms” switch and the days you save in the day finder are kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone and in the storage it shares with its own widgets. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Calculations

Lunar dates, solar terms, festivals and the almanac notes are calculated on your device from the date. The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, calendar or any other permission.

Purchases

The Premium subscription and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks the purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not access the advertising identifier.

Backups

If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, the app's data is included in your backup under your Apple Account settings, as for other apps.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can remove saved days in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a later version adds usage statistics, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
